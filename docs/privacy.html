CardDue Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

CardDue does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

The card nicknames, due days and paid status you enter are stored only on your device. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it does not connect to any server. If your device backups are turned on, this data may be included in them, under your own Apple account.

Reminders are local notifications created on your device. You can turn them off in the app or in the iOS Settings app.

Deleting the app deletes its data from your device.

Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
