CardDue Support

CardDue is a simple checklist for credit card due dates. Every card returns to unpaid on the 1st of each month.

Questions and problems

Please open an issue on GitHub.

Why didn't I get a reminder?

Check that reminders are on in the app's Settings, and that notifications are allowed for CardDue in the iOS Settings app. Open the app at least once every few months so it can schedule upcoming reminders.
